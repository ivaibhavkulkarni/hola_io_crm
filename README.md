# 📱 Mobile Repair & Retail CRM (hola-io)

A **Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system** designed for mobile repair and retail businesses. This application streamlines business operations with features like **billing, job sheet management, inventory tracking, analytics, customer management, and real-time repair status updates via APIs**.

---

## 🚀 Features

* **Billing System** – Generate and manage invoices seamlessly.
* **Job Sheets** – Track repair orders with detailed job sheets.
* **Inventory Management** – Monitor stock levels and manage spare parts efficiently.
* **Analytics Dashboard** – Get insights into business performance.
* **Customer Management** – Store and access customer details quickly.
* **Repair Status Updates** – Customers can check the real-time status of their repairs via APIs.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend**: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)
* **Backend**: [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/)
* **Database**: [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/)
* **APIs**: RESTful APIs for data exchange and customer updates

---
